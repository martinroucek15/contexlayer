# Customer Success Playbook

## Business question
Revenue has been growing, but customer satisfaction has started to decline. Leadership wants to understand whether growth is creating pressure on the customer experience.

## Customer satisfaction definition
Customer satisfaction is measured using NPS.

- Healthy: 8.0 or higher
- Watch zone: 7.0 to 7.9
- Critical: below 7.0

## Support operations rules
Average ticket resolution time is the main operational health metric.

- Target: under 8 hours
- Watch zone: 8 to 10 hours
- Overloaded: above 10 hours

When average resolution time is above 10 hours, Customer Success expects NPS to decline because customers wait longer for help.

## Business context
A Q2 acquisition push increased new customers and enterprise share. Support headcount did not increase during the same period.

## Leadership guidance
Do not celebrate revenue growth alone if NPS is falling. If support is overloaded, the recommended action is to slow aggressive acquisition, prioritize support capacity, and protect retention.
